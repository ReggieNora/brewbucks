Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about BrewBucks, our coffee, and our NFT membership.

General

BrewBucks is a premium coffee brand that combines specialty coffee with a digital membership program. We sell high-quality seasonal blends and offer an NFT Mug Membership that gives you permanent perks like discounts, early access, and exclusive products.

We source beans from independent farms in Colombia, Ethiopia, Guatemala, Kenya, Rwanda, Indonesia, and Brazil. Our head roaster visits farms personally to ensure quality and ethical practices.

Currently we ship within the United States. International shipping is coming soon. Join our email list to be the first to know when we expand.

NFT & Web3

An NFT (non-fungible token) is a digital item stored on a blockchain. In our case, it's a digital coffee mug that acts as your membership card. It proves you're a member and unlocks your perks automatically. Think of it like a digital loyalty card that you truly own.

Our NFT Mugs are on the Solana blockchain. Solana is fast, affordable, and widely supported by wallets and marketplaces.

We recommend Phantom wallet. It's free, takes about two minutes to set up, and works with Solana. You can use it as a browser extension or mobile app.

Orders & Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days within the continental US. Subscription orders ship free. We roast to order, so your coffee is always fresh.

Since coffee is perishable, we can't accept returns on opened bags. If there's an issue with your order, reach out to us and we'll make it right.
